Privacy Policy for Educational Apps

Effective July 9, 2026 · Last updated July 9, 2026

This policy covers educational apps published by Prameya LLC that do not require accounts, do not accept personal uploads, and store learning progress on your device unless an app-specific policy says otherwise.

Full policy text (authoritative Markdown): PRIVACY.md on GitHub.

1. Our commitment

We design educational apps to minimize data collection. We do not sell personal information.

2. Information we process

On your device

  • Learning progress (lessons completed, scores, stars, bookmarks)
  • Simple preferences (onboarding, display settings)
  • Practice answers that never leave the device, if offered

What we do not collect for these apps

  • Accounts, names, or contact details (except if you email support)
  • Precise location, photos, microphone, contacts, or messages
  • Health, biometric, financial, or government ID data
  • User content uploaded to our servers

Advertising (only if the app shows ads)

Free apps may use third-party ad networks such as Google AdMob, which may process device and advertising data (subject to your platform permissions). See Google’s Privacy Policy and partner sites notice.

3. Your choices

  • iOS Tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Android ads: reset or limit advertising ID in system settings
  • Local data: uninstall the app or use any in-app reset

4. Children

Apps covered here are for a general learning audience and are not directed primarily at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 on our servers.
