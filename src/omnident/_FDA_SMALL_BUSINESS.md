## 1. What this is, in one paragraph

FDA charges a user fee for a De Novo classification request. If FDA has formally determined that your
company is a "small business" for the relevant federal fiscal year, you pay **25% of the standard De Novo
fee** instead of 100%. The determination is requested on a single free form (Form FDA 3602N), it costs
**$0** to file, FDA's stated review target is **60 calendar days**, and one determination covers **every**
eligible submission your company makes during that one fiscal year. It is not automatic, it is not
retroactive, and if you file a De Novo before FDA issues your Small Business Decision number, you pay the
full fee and **FDA will not refund the difference**.

**$286,530.**

Also discounted by the same determination, if you use it: a 513(g) Request for Information is
**$8,596 standard / $4,298 small business** in FY2027.

**What is NOT discounted, and what is NOT waived — read this before you build a budget:**

- **There is no "$0" outcome for a De Novo.** FDA's one-time "first premarket application/report" fee
  waiver (the benefit tied to the $30 million threshold) is defined as the first **PMA (including Modular
  PMA), BLA, PDP, or PMR**. A De Novo request is none of those. The best possible outcome for a De Novo is
  the 25% rate — $47,755 — never zero. Do not plan against a waiver.
- **The annual establishment registration fee is not eligible for a small business reduction.** FDA states
  this explicitly. FY2027 rate: **$13,785**, flat, for everyone. See section 8.

## 4. What to gather (start here)

1. **The company's federal (U.S.) income tax return — complete, signed, dated.**
   This is the hard requirement and the most likely thing to delay you. FDA: a U.S. business cannot qualify
   as a small business if it has not submitted a Federal (U.S.) income tax return. What FDA needs:
   - An **identical signed copy of the entire original** federal return for the most recent tax year, or a
     copy of the **e-file form** submitted to the IRS — either way it must carry the **signature and the
     date of signature of an officer, partner, or member** of the company. For an LLC, a managing
     **member's** signature satisfies this.
   - **Which return** depends on how prameyallc is taxed: single-member LLC treated as a disregarded entity
     → the owner's complete signed **Form 1040 with Schedule C** identifying the business and its gross
     receipts; multi-member LLC → **Form 1065**; corporate election → **Form 1120 / 1120-S**.
   - **Do not send state returns.** FDA cannot accept a state or foreign return in place of a federal one.
   - **If the company has been in business less than a year:** FDA accepts a return covering **less than
     one year**, with the covered dates identified on the return, **plus documentation identifying the
     business's formation** (e.g. the state certificate of formation / articles of organization).
   - **If you are on extension:** you may use the most recent return filed prior to the extension, and you
     should also include **IRS Form 7004** (Application for Automatic Extension of Time to File Certain
     Business Income Tax, Information, and Other Returns).
   - **For an FY2027 request:** the most recent tax year is **2026**, except that if you file the request
     **before April 15, 2027** and have not yet filed the 2026 return, you may use **tax year 2025**.
   - **If no federal return exists for any period, the determination cannot be granted.** Get the
     accountant moving now — this is the critical path, not the FDA form.

2. **A complete list of affiliates, with each one's gross receipts or sales.**
   "Affiliate" is statutory (FD&C Act §737(13)) and broad: a business entity related to another if,
   directly or indirectly, **(a) one controls, or has the power to control, the other**, or **(b) a third
   party controls, or has power to control, both**. Note prong (b): if the owner of prameyallc also
   controls other LLCs or corporations, **those entities' gross receipts must be added in**, even if they
   have nothing to do with medical devices. The test is control or the *power* to control — not ownership
   percentage, not accounting consolidation. Aggregation is mandatory: your receipts plus all affiliates'
   receipts must total **$100,000,000 or less** for the reduced-fee benefit.
   Each **U.S. affiliate** needs its own separate federal income tax return in the package.
   Failing to disclose a parent, partner, or affiliate is a **false certification**; consequences include
   suspension of small business status, suspension of review of pending applications until the full fee is
   paid, collection of unpaid fees, other legal action, and criminal exposure under **18 U.S.C. § 1001**.

3. **Your Org ID (Organization ID Number).**
   A number generated by the **FDA User Fee System** that uniquely identifies your business there. It is
   **not** your EIN, not your registration number, not your TIN. If prameyallc already has a user fee
   account, **use the existing Org ID — do not create a second one**; a duplicate is a common cause of a
   mismatch between the decision letter and the De Novo cover sheet. Account creation / login:
   `https://userfees.fda.gov/OA_HTML/mdufmaCAcdLogin.jsp`.

4. **A CDRH Portal account.**
   Register at `https://ccp.fda.gov/prweb/PRAuth/selfregistration`; sign in at
   `https://ccp.fda.gov/prweb/PRAuth/app/default/extsso`. CDRH uses **Okta** for identity verification and
   single sign-on — registration and password-reset emails come from an address ending in **@okta.com**.
   They look like phishing and are not, but check the sending domain carefully.

5. **Form FDA 3602N — "MDUFA SMALL BUSINESS REQUEST."**
   Download the **current** edition from the FDA Forms database:
   `https://www.fda.gov/about-fda/reports-manuals-forms/forms`. The FY2027 Federal Register notice tells
   applicants twice to use "the most current FDA Form" from that database. The edition verified for this
   document has the footer **"FORM FDA 3602N (07/26)"**, OMB No. 0910-0508, expiration July 31, 2028.
   **Do not use a copy from a third-party website or a saved older copy** — superseded editions circulate
   and filing one invites an "On hold" status that burns weeks you do not have.
   *(The Forms database table is rendered by JavaScript — open it in a real browser, not a download tool.)*

   > ⚠️ **VERIFY-BEFORE-RELIANCE — form lineage.** FDA's guidance webpage states the guidance "is also
   > being updated to reflect the planned replacement of Forms 3602 and 3602A with a single combined form
   > (Form 3602N)"; the guidance PDF itself says only that FDA "intends to combine and update the forms
   > FDA-3602 and FDA-3602A" and never uses the string "3602N". No currently retrievable FDA primary
   > source labels the retired 3602 as "domestic" and 3602A as "foreign." What matters operationally is
   > confirmed and unambiguous: **3602N is the single form used by both U.S. and foreign businesses today.**
   > If these citations go into a regulatory file, verify the attribution.

## 5. Execution checklist, in order

1. **Confirm the tax return exists.** Call the accountant this week. Establish which federal return has
   been filed, get a complete signed and dated PDF, and confirm the signer is an officer, partner, or
   member. If nothing has been filed, ask what it takes to file now (a low- or zero-revenue return still
   satisfies the requirement) — nothing else in this plan can proceed without it.
2. **Enumerate affiliates.** Write down every entity that prameyallc controls, that controls prameyallc, or
   that shares a controlling person with prameyallc. Get each one's gross receipts or sales and, for U.S.
   entities, their federal returns.
3. **Locate or create the Org ID** in the FDA User Fee System. Record it exactly.
4. **Create the CDRH Portal account** (Okta). Do this early — account setup is not instant.
5. **Download Form FDA 3602N** fresh from the FDA Forms database; confirm the footer edition before filling
   it in.
6. **Complete the form:**
   - Enter the fiscal year at the top: **2027**.
   - **Section I** — company information; **item 4 is the certifier, who must be an employee of
     prameyallc**. Items 8–11 are the Primary Contact and *may* be a non-employee (e.g. a consultant), but
     a consultant can never sign the certification. Use a mailbox that is actually monitored — FDA's
     decision letter comes by email.
   - **Item 12** — tick the threshold box(es) that are truthfully applicable. The one that matters for the
     De Novo fee is **$100,000,000 or less (in U.S. dollars)**.
   - **Section II** — one line per entity (your business plus each affiliate), with firm name, country,
     taxpayer ID and gross receipts; **line 21 is the total** of lines 1–20 and is the number FDA tests
     against the threshold. Certifier initials the Section II attestation.
   - **Section III** — National Taxing Authority Certification, **for non-U.S. businesses and affiliates
     only**. A U.S. LLC with no foreign affiliates **leaves this blank**. (For anyone who does need it:
     the business completes fields 1–6 and the **National Taxing Authority** completes and signs fields
     7–12 and affixes its official seal — a separate Section III per foreign entity. That is a
     multi-week dependency if it ever applies.)
   - **Section IV** — registration fee waiver request. **Leave blank this year**; see section 8 for why it
     is impossible in a first registration year.
   - Sign and date.
7. **Assemble the package:** completed 3602N (with Org ID) + complete signed federal return + a separate
   federal return for each U.S. affiliate (+ Form 7004 if on extension, + formation documents if using a
   short-period return).
8. **Submit through the CDRH Portal.** Electronic submission is mandatory — as of November 1, 2024 all
   Small Business Requests must be submitted electronically. In the portal: **Create a Request** → **Start**
   next to **Small Business Determination request** → complete the fields, upload every document, submit.

   > ⚠️ **VERIFY-BEFORE-RELIANCE.** Older editions of the form's printed instructions have contained a
   > "print, sign and mail" paragraph left over from the previous forms. If you see any mailing address in
   > the instructions, **ignore it** — the electronic-only requirement is current FDA policy and governs.
   > If in doubt, confirm with DICE before mailing anything.

9. **Expect an automated confirmation email** that the request was received. Then watch the portal status:
   **Processing** (received, virus scan done) → **Reviewing** → possibly **On hold** (reviewers flagged
   issues and need more from you) → **Finished** (Approved or Denied). **"On hold" is the status that
   quietly eats your schedule — respond the same day.**
10. **Receive the decision letter** by email and via the portal. If you qualify, it contains your **Small
    Business Decision number** and states which reduction(s) you were granted. FDA's stated review target
    is **60 calendar days from receipt** (a target, not a guarantee — an "On hold" restarts the practical
    clock).
11. **Only then, and only on or after October 1, 2026:** create the Medical Device User Fee Cover Sheet
    (**Form FDA 3601**) in the FDA User Fee System, choose the **FY2027** application-date option, enter the
    Small Business Decision number, and pay **$47,755**. The company name and Org ID on the cover sheet must
    match the decision letter, and **prameyallc must be the named applicant** on the De Novo.
12. **Pay by ACH or wire, not by credit card.** Credit card is available only for balances under $25,000
    (cover-sheet card transactions are capped at $24,999.99); $47,755 is far above that. Include the PIN
    from the cover sheet with the payment. Allow settlement time (see section 6).
13. **Submit the eSTAR De Novo** once payment is recognized. Repeat steps 11–13 for the second device — one
    determination, but **two cover sheets and two fees**.
14. **Diary the renewals:** the FY2028 SBR window is expected to open around **August 1, 2027**; small
    business status **expires September 30** of its fiscal year, every year.

Questions about the program go to FDA's Division of Industry and Consumer Education: **DICE@fda.hhs.gov**.

## 6. Timing and the October 1, 2026 boundary — say it plainly

**A determination is good for one fiscal year and dies on September 30 of that year.** There is no
12-month clock; a determination granted in August 2026 for FY2026 would have expired September 30, 2026.

**Which fiscal year's determination a submission needs:**

| When your De Novo lands | Fiscal year | Determination needed | Available to you? |
|---|---|---|---|
| On or before **2026-09-30** | FY2026 | **FY2026** SBD | **No** — window closed 2026-08-01. Full fee $173,782. |
| **2026-10-01** through **2027-09-30** | FY2027 | **FY2027** SBD | **Yes** — window open now. $47,755. |
| On or after **2027-10-01** | FY2028 | **FY2028** SBD | Requires a new request in the FY2028 window (expected to open ~2027-08-01). |

**How FDA decides which side of the boundary you are on — this is the trap.** From the FY2027 Federal
Register notice: *"The later of the date that the application is received in the reviewing center's
document room or the date the U.S. Treasury recognizes the payment determines whether the fee rates for
FY 2026 or FY 2027 apply. FDA must receive the correct fee at the time that an application is submitted, or
the application will not be accepted for filing or review."*

Consequences you must design around:

- It is the **later** of the two events. Paying early does not lock in a rate if the application lands in
  the next fiscal year, and vice versa.
- "Treasury recognizes the payment" is **not** the day you initiate an ACH transfer. Build in settlement
  lag before any boundary date.
- The cover sheet offers two date-range choices until October 1, 2026 — one for applications and fees
  received on or before 2026-09-30 (FY2026 rates) and one for on or after 2026-10-01 (FY2027 rates).
  **Picking the wrong one is a common and expensive misclick.**
- **Do not file either De Novo before October 1, 2026, and do not file either one before the FY2027
  Decision number is in hand — whichever is later.**

**Two independent 60-day facts, both real:**
- Statutory: an applicant seeking a fee reduction must submit evidence of qualification **at least 60 days
  before the fee at issue is due** (FD&C Act §§738(a)(3)(B)(ii)(V), 738(d)(2)(D), 738(e)(2)(D)).
- Operational: FDA reviews the request **within 60 calendar days of receipt** and then issues the decision
  letter with the Decision number.

Filing the FY2027 request in the second week of August 2026 puts the expected decision in **mid-October
2026** — just past the fiscal-year rollover, which is exactly when you need it. Every week of delay pushes
the first De Novo filing back by the same week.

**One more horizon note:** MDUFA V runs through **September 30, 2027**. FY2028 fee amounts and program
mechanics are not a safe extrapolation from FY2027. If either De Novo could slip past September 2027, plan
for a fresh determination and unknown rates.

## 8. Establishment registration — a separate bill that this does not reduce

- prameyallc, as a company that develops the specifications for a device distributed under its own name and
  does no manufacturing, is a **Specification Developer** — required to **register**, **list** each device,
  and **pay** the annual establishment fee (21 CFR 807.20(a)(1)).
- **Do not register yet.** FDA: if your device requires premarket clearance or approval, you must wait until
  the premarket submission is cleared or approved to register the establishment and list the device. Initial
  registration and listing are submitted **within 30 days** of beginning the activity or putting the device
  into commercial distribution. Registering before authorization creates a bill and a compliance problem for
  no benefit.
- **The registration fee is flat and cannot be reduced.** FDA: *"The establishment registration fee is not
  eligible for a reduced small business fee."* FY2027: **$13,785** (FY2026 was $11,423).
- **The only relief is a full waiver, and it is out of reach in year one.** It requires all three of:
  gross receipts (with affiliates) of **$1,000,000 or less**; **financial hardship** as determined by FDA
  (the guidance's worked example is evidence of active bankruptcy); and **proof that you paid the
  registration fee in a prior fiscal year**. The third condition makes it impossible in your first
  registration year by construction, the waiver **excludes the initial registration**, and FDA "may, but is
  not required to," grant it.
- **Good news:** the fee is per establishment per fiscal year, **not per device** — one registration covers
  both OmniDerm and OmniDent.
- **Payment precedes registration**, and the annual renewal window is **October 1 – December 31** each year.
  If you ever do pursue the registration-fee waiver in a later year, note it has an **earlier, different
  deadline**: for actively registered firms FDA accepts SBRs for the registration fee waiver through
  **October 31**.

**Budget line for year one of commercial distribution: $13,785, unavoidable.**

## 9. Other things that cost money or time if missed

- **The De Novo fee is non-refundable once the request is accepted for review — including if FDA declines
  it.** FDA will not refund when the request is accepted for review, when an eSTAR passes technical
  screening, or when an accepted request is declined. FDA will refund in narrower cases (payment made when
  a statutory exception applied; withdrawal after failure to supply a valid eCopy/eSTAR; withdrawal where
  acceptance criteria are not met; withdrawal of an eSTAR that failed technical screening and is on hold).
  Refund requests must be submitted in writing **no later than 180 days after the fee is due**.
  → Two De Novos means up to **$95,510 at risk with no refund if they fail**. A **Pre-Submission (Q-Sub)
  carries no user fee** and is the cheap way to test the pathway and the evidence package first; a 513(g)
  ($4,298 at the small business rate) is the cheaper paid way to buy a classification answer.
- **There is a statutory fee exemption worth knowing exists:** no De Novo user fee if the submission is for
  a device **intended solely for a pediatric population** (FD&C Act §738(a)(2)(B)(v)(I)), or for a state or
  federal government entity whose device will not be distributed commercially. Almost certainly not your
  indication — and narrowing an indication purely to avoid a fee is a bad trade — but be aware of it.
- **Small business status is not transferable.** A consultant who files on your behalf is not the applicant
  and does not qualify. If prameyallc is acquired, the acquirer needs its own determination. The firm
  information and Org ID on the decision letter must match the submission.
- **Renew annually.** If either De Novo is still pending into FY2028, or you later file supplements or
  510(k)s, you need a fresh determination for each fiscal year, filed inside that year's window.

  > ⚠️ **VERIFY-BEFORE-RELIANCE.** FDA's guidance PDF and the live SBD webpage describe the annual window
  > with slightly different closing dates. The **webpage is the more current instrument** — rely on it, and
  > re-read it each July before the window turns over rather than assuming last year's dates.

  > ⚠️ **VERIFY-BEFORE-RELIANCE.** It is a reasonable reading that filing these two De Novo requests does
  > **not** consume prameyallc's one-time "first premarket application/report" waiver, because that waiver
  > is defined solely by reference to PMA / Modular PMA / BLA / PDP / PMR. FDA has not said this explicitly.
  > **Confirm with DICE or counsel before relying on it** if a PMA is ever contemplated.
