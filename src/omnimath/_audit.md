## Corrections to prior claims

- CLAIM (docs/PRIVACY.md, 'Advertising' section): 'If ads are enabled in your build of the App…'. FALSE AS FRAMED. Ads are not conditional. `AdMobConfig.useProductionAds = true` and OmniMathApp.swift calls `AdConsent.requestConsentAndStartAds()` in the root `.task` on every launch. There is no build, setting or user choice in which OmniMath does not show ads. ACCURATE: 'OmniMath displays banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. Ad loading begins when you open the app.'
- CLAIM (docs/PRIVACY.md, Summary): 'Where you allow it, the App may request App Tracking Transparency permission.' MISLEADING. The ATT request is unconditional whenever status is `.notDetermined` — `AdConsent.requestTrackingAuthorizationIfNeeded()` runs at launch on every fresh install. ACCURATE: 'The first time you open OmniMath, iOS shows Apple's tracking permission dialog.'
- CLAIM (docs/PRIVACY.md, Children's privacy): 'We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 on our own servers.' TECHNICALLY TRUE BUT EMPTY AND THEREFORE DECEPTIVE IN EFFECT. Prameya operates no servers at all, so the sentence excludes exactly the collection that actually happens — Google's ad requests, which fire at launch before any age question. ACCURATE: say that we collect nothing directly because we have no server, AND that the advertising identifier can be transmitted to Google for any user including a child, AND give parents the concrete controls (Tracking setting, Screen Time).
- CLAIM (docs/PRIVACY.md, Data sharing and sale): 'We do not sell your personal information.' INCOMPLETE, AND INCOMPLETE IN THE DIRECTION THAT FAVOURS US. It omits 'sharing' for cross-context behavioural advertising, which is a separate defined term under CPRA and is exactly what happens when ATT is authorised. ACCURATE: 'We do not sell personal information for money. When you allow tracking, an advertising identifier is disclosed to Google for personalised advertising; California law calls that sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising, and some state laws would call it a sale. The opt-out is to deny tracking.'
- CLAIM (docs/APP_STORE.md §5 label table): 'Everything else (contacts, health, LOCATION, financial, browsing, purchases, user content) — Not collected.' FALSE for location. GoogleMobileAds.framework/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy declares `NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypeCoarseLocation` with `Linked = true`, for third-party advertising and analytics. ACCURATE: coarse location IS collected by the ad SDK — derived from IP address, not from GPS — and linked to the user. The app itself uses no CoreLocation and requests no location permission; both facts must be stated together.
- CLAIM (docs/APP_REVIEW_INFORMATION.md, lines 127-129): 'App privacy nutrition labels in App Store Connect have been updated to disclose that data may be used to Track You.' UNSUBSTANTIATED. ISSUES.md MATH-010 records that this was never re-derived from the actual dependency graph. A label asserted from memory is both an App Review problem and an FTC Act §5 problem.

ALL THREE ARE FALSE FOR OMNIMATH. It vendors Google Mobile Ads 12.14.0 and the User Messaging Platform as binary xcframeworks, it transmits on every launch, it cannot answer 'Data Not Collected', and `NSPrivacyTracking` must be `true` with Google's tracking domains listed. ## Open questions

- IN-APP PRIVACY LINK (MATH-026): the shipping build has no Settings screen at all, so there is no in-app link to this policy. App Review Guideline 5.1.1(i) expects the link inside the app. Confirm the Wave 2 Settings screen has landed before publishing, and before submission.
- UMP RE-CONSENT (MATH-009): `presentPrivacyOptionsForm` appears nowhere in the repo. The policy says consent can be withdrawn as easily as it was given, which is a GDPR Art. 7(3) requirement. That sentence is not yet true of the shipping build. Ship the 'Ad privacy choices' entry point or amend the paragraph.
- APP PRIVACY MANIFEST (MATH-002): there is no `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` for the OmniMath app target — the only ones present are inside the vendored Google frameworks. It must declare the `UserDefaults` required-reason (CA92.1), `NSPrivacyTracking = true`, Google's current tracking domains, and the collected data types. App Store Connect rejects the upload without it. Take the tracking-domain list from Google's own page for SDK 12.14.0; do not guess it.
- APP STORE PRIVACY LABELS vs GOOGLE'S MANIFEST: docs/APP_STORE.md §5 says location is 'Not collected', but GoogleMobileAds' own manifest declares Coarse Location as collected AND linked to the user. Re-derive every label answer from the two framework manifests before submission, and correct the location answer if the labels currently say otherwise.
- CHILD-DIRECTEDNESS DETERMINATION (MATH-006 / OQ-M04): the children's section of this policy rests on OmniMath being a general-audience app. There is still no dated, retained intended-audience determination anywhere in the repo. Record it, with an author and a date, and re-affirm it whenever store copy changes. The 2025 COPPA amendments expressly weigh marketing materials, representations to consumers, and reviews.
- AD-FREE OPTION: the policy states there is no paid ad-free version. Confirm that is still the intended shipping configuration — if an ad-free purchase is ever added, this policy needs rewriting, not patching.
- APP-ADS.TXT (MATH-012) is not hosted. Not a privacy-policy issue, but the developer website declared in App Store Connect is the same domain that will host this policy — decide the domain once and host both there.
- UK ICO CHILDREN'S CODE (OQ-M09): its scope test is whether the service is 'likely to be accessed by' UK under-18s, which is a lower bar than COPPA's 'directed to children'. An ad-supported maths education app is plausibly in scope. This policy does not claim Children's Code conformance. Counsel should decide whether conformance work is needed for UK distribution.
- TEXAS SB 2420 / DECLARED AGE RANGE (MATH-008): not implemented. If Apple's age-signal APIs are adopted, the app will receive an age category from Apple, which is itself a new data flow and would need a line in this policy.
- CCPA APPLICABILITY: the policy honours CCPA rights while noting Prameya may fall below the statutory thresholds. Counsel should confirm whether that hedge is the position the owner wants stated publicly, or whether to drop the sentence and simply honour the rights.
- PRIVACY POLICY URL MIGRATION (MATH-023): App Store Connect, AdMob, README.md, docs/ADMOB_SETUP.md, docs/APP_STORE_LISTING.md and docs/APP_REVIEW_INFORMATION.md all still point at github.com/prameyallc/edu-app-privacy/blob/main/PRIVACY.md. All six need updating to https://prameyallc.github.io/privacy/omnimath/ at the same time, or the App Store listing will link to a policy that contradicts this one.
- docs/PRIVACY.md IN THIS REPO must be replaced with this text (or reduced to a pointer). Leaving the July 2026 draft in place means two policies with different contents exist for the same app.
