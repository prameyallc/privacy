# Privacy Policy for Educational Apps

**Effective date:** July 9, 2026  
**Last updated:** July 9, 2026  
**Publisher:** Prameya LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”)

This Privacy Policy applies to **educational mobile and software applications** published by Prameya LLC that:

- are designed for learning, teaching, reference, or skill practice;  
- **do not** require users to create an account or sign in;  
- **do not** allow users to upload, post, or submit personal documents, photos of people, health data, financial data, government IDs, or other regulated personal information; and  
- store learning progress (if any) **on the user’s device** unless a specific app’s product page says otherwise.

Where a particular app has its own privacy policy linked in the App Store or Play Store listing, that app-specific policy controls for that app. Otherwise, this policy applies.

---

## 1. Our commitment

We build educational apps so you can learn with confidence. We design them to **minimize data collection**. We do **not** sell personal information.

---

## 2. Information we process

### 2.1 Information that stays on your device

Depending on the app, the following may be stored **locally on your device** only:

- Learning progress (e.g. completed lessons, scores, stars, bookmarks)  
- Simple preferences (e.g. onboarding completed, display settings)  
- Content you type **inside the app for practice** that never leaves the device (e.g. answers to an exercise), if the app offers that feature  

We do **not** operate a user profile database for these apps. Local data is under your control (device backups, uninstall, or any in-app reset).

### 2.2 Information we do not collect for these apps

For apps covered by this policy, we do **not** intentionally collect or ask you to provide:

- Name, email, phone number, or postal address (except if you email us for support)  
- Account credentials  
- Precise location  
- Photos, videos, or microphone audio of you  
- Contacts, calendar, or messages  
- Health, biometric, or financial information  
- Government-issued ID numbers  
- User-generated content uploaded to our servers  

### 2.3 When you contact us

If you email support, we receive whatever you send (typically your email address and message) so we can respond. We use that only for support and related legal compliance.

### 2.4 Advertising (if enabled in a specific app)

Some free educational apps may display ads through third-party advertising networks (for example, **Google AdMob**).

If ads are present, the ad provider may automatically collect technical and advertising-related data, such as:

- Device type and operating system  
- IP address and approximate location derived from IP  
- Advertising identifiers (subject to your platform permission, e.g. Apple’s App Tracking Transparency)  
- Ad performance and diagnostic data  

We do not control every practice of third-party ad networks. Please review their policies as well, including:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)  
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

If an app does **not** include advertising, this section does not apply to that app.

### 2.5 Analytics and crash reporting (if enabled)

Some apps may use privacy-respecting analytics or crash diagnostics (for example, aggregated crash logs) to improve stability. When used, such tools may collect device model, OS version, and crash stack traces. They are not used to build marketing profiles of learners.

---

## 3. How we use information

We use information only to:

- Provide and improve educational features  
- Remember on-device progress and preferences  
- Show ads in free ad-supported builds (when enabled)  
- Fix bugs and improve reliability  
- Respond to support requests  
- Comply with law and protect our rights  

We do **not** use educational apps covered by this policy to sell personal information or to build off-app advertising profiles ourselves.

---

## 4. Tracking and your choices (Apple / Google)

### Apple App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, an app may ask for permission to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites for personalized advertising.

- You may choose **Ask App Not to Track**.  
- You can change this later in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**.  
- Ads may still appear with limited personalization depending on the ad network and platform rules.

### Android advertising ID

On Android, you can reset or limit your advertising ID in system settings where available.

### Uninstall and local data

Uninstalling the app removes local app data (subject to your device backup settings).

---

## 5. Sharing of information

We share information only as needed:

| Recipient | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Advertising partners (if the app shows ads) | Deliver and measure ads |
| Infrastructure / OS vendors (Apple, Google) | App distribution, OS services, optional crash tooling |
| Service providers | Only if needed to run the app or support (bound by appropriate terms) |
| Legal authorities | When required by law or to protect safety and rights |

We do **not sell** personal information.

---

## 6. Children and education

These apps are intended for a **general learning audience** (students, educators, self-learners, and adults). They are **not directed primarily at children under 13**.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 on our servers for apps covered by this policy. Parents or guardians who believe a child has contacted us with personal information may email us to request deletion of that correspondence.

If you use an app in a classroom, the school or district may have additional policies; this document describes only our practices for the software we publish.

---

## 7. Data retention

- **On-device data:** retained until you clear it, reset progress, or uninstall the app (or until device backups restore it).  
- **Support emails:** retained as long as needed to handle your request and for ordinary business records.  
- **Ad/network data:** retained according to the third party’s policies.

---

## 8. Security

We design apps so core learning progress does not need to leave your device. No system is perfectly secure; protect your device with a passcode or biometrics and keep your OS updated.

---

## 9. International users

We are based in the United States. If you use our apps from another country, any limited information you send us (e.g. support email) may be processed in the United States or other locations where our providers operate.

---

## 10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EEA/UK), you may have rights to request access, deletion, or information about certain data practices.

Because apps covered by this policy **do not maintain user accounts or server-side learner profiles**, many rights requests are limited to:

- Support correspondence we hold by email  
- Guidance on clearing on-device data  
- Questions about advertising partners  

To exercise a privacy request, contact us (see below). We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable rights.

**Do Not Sell / Share:** We do not sell personal information. For advertising opt-outs, use your device tracking/ad settings and the ad provider’s tools.

---

## 11. Third-party links and services

Apps may link to external websites (e.g. documentation). Those sites have their own privacy practices. This policy does not cover third-party sites.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Material changes may also be noted in an app update description or on this page. Continued use of the apps after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy where permitted by law.
