OmniPhysics Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 August 2026
Publisher: Prameya LLC ("Prameya", "we", "us")
App: OmniPhysics for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro — bundle ID legal.prameya.OmniPhysics
Contact: admin@prameya.legal
Scope: This policy covers the OmniPhysics app and nothing else. Prameya's other apps have their own policies, because they work differently. Index: https://prameyallc.github.io/privacy/

The short version

  • No ads. No App Tracking Transparency. No third-party analytics.
  • Progress stays on this device. Optional Apple iCloud (CloudKit) sync only if you are signed in and the capability is on.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • HIPAA and FERPA do not apply. This is not a school or health record.
  • There is nothing to buy. Unused in-app purchase code was removed; there is no paywall.

What OmniPhysics is

OmniPhysics is an educational physics app (classical mechanics and waves) for learners 13 and older. It is not in the App Store Kids Category and is not directed at children under 13.

What the app processes

Data Where Purpose
Display name you choose Device (and iCloud if enabled) Personalize Profile
Lesson progress, XP, stars, streaks, hearts, achievements Device (and iCloud if enabled) Save learning
Settings (sound, haptics) Device (and iCloud if enabled) Remember preferences

We do not collect precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or off-device analytics. We do not collect photos, camera or microphone input; the app plays bundled UI sounds and records nothing.

There is no account of ours. There is no third-party SDK.

iCloud sync

When CloudKit is available and you are signed into iCloud:

  • Progress may sync across your devices signed into the same Apple ID.
  • Sync is handled by Apple's infrastructure; we do not operate a separate user account server.
  • You can keep using the app offline.

If CloudKit is not available (not signed in, or the container is not provisioned), the app runs in local-only mode. Debug builds omit CloudKit entitlements and stay local-only.

What OmniPhysics does not do

  • No ads of any kind.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA), no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No third-party analytics or crash reporter.
  • No camera, photos, microphone, contacts, or location APIs.
  • No health, fitness or medical data of any kind.
  • No on-device AI model and no model download.
  • No paywall, subscription prompt, or in-app purchase screen.

Children

Not Kids Category. Not directed at children under 13. We do not use progress data for advertising.

Your choices

  • Toggle sound and haptics in Profile.
  • Reset progress from Profile (erases local progress records).
  • Manage iCloud storage for the app in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud.

Data retention

Progress remains until you reset it, delete the app, or remove the app's iCloud data.
